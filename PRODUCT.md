# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Next.js (App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS) on Vercel's free tier, talking to a FastAPI + Postgres/pgvector backend through a same-origin `/api` rewrite. Chosen for zero-cost hosting, server rendering of public pages, and first-party session cookies.

## Users

Indian engineering students and freshers (the founder, friends and juniors first) hunting internships and new-grad roles across software engineering and its variants: backend, ML, LLM/AI, infra, data engineering, data science. They target Indian cities (Mumbai, Bengaluru first) plus remote roles in India and worldwide. They juggle several resume versions, apply to many roles, and check job sources daily, usually on a phone between classes or on a laptop in the evening. Experienced engineers can use it too through level options, but are not the design target.

## Product Purpose

Rolecall crawls thousands of fresh tech roles every day from company career boards, understands each one (level, role, skills, location, pay), and ranks them against the user's resume and behaviour, with a reason for every match. It also tracks applications and has an agent that can search, compare, track and set alerts in chat. Success: a user finds roles worth applying to in minutes instead of trawling ten sites, trusts why each was suggested, and keeps their whole hunt in one place.

## Positioning

Recommendations are explainable and grounded: every match shows the skills it matched, the level fit, red flags like "asks for 3+ years", and where the job came from (the company's own board). Ranking quality is measured publicly with offline evals. It is built for the Indian student hunt specifically: India-first locations, internships and new-grad roles, stipends in INR.

## Operating Context

- Daily check-in on a personalised feed; quick save/dismiss triage, often on mobile.
- Several resumes per user (e.g. SWE vs ML) that switch which feed they see.
- Application tracker (saved, applied, OA, interview, offer, rejected) with Excel export, used alongside spreadsheets the users already keep.
- Email digest of new matches and saved-search alerts.
- Chat agent for multi-step asks ("find 5 remote ML internships and save the best two").
- Admin metrics and a public evals page, used by the founder to show the system works (also part of a job application portfolio).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- $0 budget: free-tier hosting and free-tier LLM APIs (Groq, Gemini, Cerebras, OpenRouter) or local models; every LLM feature degrades gracefully when no key or quota is available.
- Google sign-in only. Resume PDFs are parsed and discarded; contact details are redacted.
- Sources: Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, SmartRecruiters, Recruitee, Workday, HN Who's Hiring, Remotive, Arbeitnow, RemoteOK, and JSON-LD career pages. No LinkedIn/Naukri/Internshala scraping.
- Rolecall cannot apply on the user's behalf.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Rolecall (a play on "roll call"). Voice: direct, friendly, peer-to-peer, honest about fit; never hype.

## Evidence on Hand

Live index of real postings (thousands of open tech jobs from ~240 boards), live stats endpoint, eval results generated by the system itself. No testimonials, user counts beyond real data, partner logos, or press exist; do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. Every recommendation explains itself; no black-box scores.
2. Respect the user's time: triage in seconds, depth on demand.
3. Honest over flattering: show gaps and red flags, not just matches.
4. Works for free, for everyone: graceful degradation when AI is unavailable.
5. The user owns their data: export everything, delete everything.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Full keyboard support for triage; works well on low-end Android phones and slow connections.
