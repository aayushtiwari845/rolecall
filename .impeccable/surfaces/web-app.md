## Scope

Whole web app (web/): landing (Persuade) plus feed, search, job detail, tracker, chat, settings, onboarding, admin, evals (Operate). One world across all of it.

## Audience and task

Indian students and freshers triaging fresh tech roles daily, mostly on phones; landing visitors decide to sign in.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The hunt as a live innings. Every role is a scorecard row of hard numbers; every new posting is called out ball by ball. Refuses the card grid with logo tiles, pill chips and a search-bar hero.

OWN-WORLD: Scoreboard pitch-black green panels with chalk-white tabular numerals and scoreboard amber; light mode is the scorer's book, cool white with green hairline rules. Red only for wickets (dismissed, rejected), green for runs (offers, strong fits). Big Shoulders Display numerals, Archivo text. Rows, not cards. Pen rings and strikes mark state in a fixed column.

STORY: Visitors see today's roles counted live, believe each match is earned by visible reasons, sign in, upload a resume and triage their scorecard daily.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Landing: left two thirds a giant scoreboard of today's counts (new roles, India roles, early-career) as huge numerals, headline beneath, amber "Get my scorecard" action; right third a live commentary column of the newest real postings.

FORM: Scorecard, seventh on the grounded list, seed 99320afa.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Memorable moment

The commentary column ticking new postings in as "balls", and the feed's match % set like a batter's score.
